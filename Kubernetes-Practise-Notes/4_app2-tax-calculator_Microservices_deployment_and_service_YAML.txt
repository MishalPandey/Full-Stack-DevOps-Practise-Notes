				  ports:
					- containerPort: 4000
		---
		apiVersion: v1
		kind: Service
		metadata:
		  name: service-b
		spec:
		  selector:
			app: service-b
		  type: ClusterIP
		  ports:
			- port: 4000
			  targetPort: 4000
			  
			  
1.1--> why used ClusterIP in place of NodePort?
       -->kyuki NodePort Locally browser me expose karta URL ko aur yaha service-b ko internally service-a call karega
	       toh hame ClusterIP chahiye hoga internal communication ke liye aur kyuki NodePort nhi use kar rahe toh nodePort: 32200 ki bhi
		   jarurat nhi yeh exposing service url ke liye hai.
		   
1.2--> containerPort: 4000 why?
       --> kyuki container ke andar 4000 pe expose ho rahi yeh service.
	   
1.3--> kubectl apply -f service-b
       --> apply the changes in service-b directory

		deployment.apps/service-b created
		service/service-b created

		kind: Service

			  targetPort: 3000
			  nodePort: 32200

      --> Ab hum http://localhost:32200/price?country=IN&amount=300 local browser ke through service-a ke endpoint ko access kar sakte esliye NodePort,
	      and nodePort: 32200 here is the port defined.
